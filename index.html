Practice Task Prompt

"Video games influence the way people behave in real life. Age restrictions should be enforced more strictly and some games should be banned completely."

Reflect on the real-life news case (e.g. young teenagers committing driving infractions after playing GTA) when taking your stance.

2 Brainstorming Arguments & Real-Life Evidence

Gather your primary points, opposing viewpoints, and real-life incidents before drafting paragraphs.

Arguments FOR Censorship/Restrictions

Why do violent/realistic games harm young minds?

Arguments AGAINST Bans / For Personal Choice

Why should games NOT be banned completely?

Real-Life Incident or Specific Example:

Include an example (e.g. news reports, high-speed car chase cases, gaming addiction studies) to validate your core claim.

3 Build Your 5-Paragraph Essay Plan

Follow the textbook essay structure (Page 27, Exercise 4) and utilize B2+ Writing Bank expressions.

Paragraph 1: Introduction, including your opinion

Hook + General Opinion + Thesis

Introduce the controversy surrounding video games and state your firm thesis statement.

Paragraph 2: Main point(s) in support of your opinion

Primary Argument

Detail your strongest argument supporting your thesis.

Paragraph 3: Additional point(s) in support + Real Example

Secondary Argument + Evidence

Provide a second supporting point and integrate your real-life case study/example.

Paragraph 4: Point(s) in support of a different opinion + Counterargument(s)

Concession & Refutation

Acknowledge the opposite side, then explain why their argument is flawed or insufficient.

Paragraph 5: Conclusion, restating and expanding your main point

Summary & Final Warning/Call to Action

Summarize arguments without repeating exact words and end with a thought-provoking conclusion.
